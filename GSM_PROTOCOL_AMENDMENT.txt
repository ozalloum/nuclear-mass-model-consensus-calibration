JPhysG-106072: GSM data-source amendment

Original provision
The frozen protocol's RQ3 specified the calcium chain Z=20, N=28..40 and
Li et al., Phys. Rev. C 102, 034302 (2020), with Coraggio et al. as a
corroborating regional reference. Numerical values were restricted to
source tables, author data or reproducible calculations; manual figure
digitization was excluded from headline numerical claims.

Change and timing
This revision admits audited vector-digitized GSM estimates for the regional
comparison. The decision was made after peer review and after the extracted
values had been inspected. RQ3 was prespecified; the acquisition method and
its sensitivity analysis were adopted during revision. The original frozen
protocol and all original global-model inputs, thresholds and scores remain
unchanged. This document does not retrospectively preregister the change.

Reason
The request for author-generated values on 3 September 2026 and a subsequent
follow-up had not yielded numerical data. The reviewer requested that RQ3 be addressed.
Vector coordinates permit reproducible reconstruction of the plotted values;
choosing this method is the author's revision decision, not a specific
reviewer instruction to digitize.

Scope and safeguards
1. GSM remains a regional structural comparison, outside the global ensemble.
2. Values are labeled vector-digitized estimates, not original numerical outputs.
3. Journal page 5 and arXiv page 11 both reproduce all 15 supplied values at
   0.01 MeV. Source hashes, coordinate records and external-PDF check are retained.
4. The main case is A=48..60; no GSM A=48 value is imputed. A=62..72 is archived
   as an extension only. All six scored methods use common measured A=50,52,54,56.
5. Core48 and core54 series stay separate. Only A=56 is measured in their overlap.
6. AME2020 estimates at A=58,60 do not enter measured-reference statistics.
7. Allowances through +/-0.10 MeV are stress tests, not confidence intervals,
   theory uncertainties or proven error bounds on the original solver values.
8. The regional comparison is retrospective, including Li's use of experimental
   55Ca information. It does not establish causality or independent forecast skill.

Files
